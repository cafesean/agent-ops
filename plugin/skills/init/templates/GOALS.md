# GOALS — the one goal list
One block per goal. `spawn.sh` parses the `### <ID> · <outcome>` heading.
- `intent:` = the owner's own words, quoted. No words yet → `— (owner to say)` and `status: confirm?`.
- Only the owner moves a goal to `confirmed`. `parked` goals cannot receive workers.
- `- tracked:` bullets = items the chief tracks under the goal.

### G1 · <outcome, e.g. "ship v1 of the billing page">
intent: "<why, in your words>"
done means: <measurable finish line + deadline>
status: confirm?
- tracked: —
